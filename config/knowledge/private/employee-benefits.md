## Health & Wellness

### Medical Insurance

**Provider:** Blue Cross Blue Shield (US), Bupa (EU), Medibank (Australia)

**Coverage Tiers:**
- Employee Only
- Employee + Spouse/Partner
- Employee + Children
- Family

**Company Contribution:**
- 90% of premium for employee
- 75% of premium for dependents

**Plans Available:**
- Platinum Plan (Low deductible, high premium)
- Gold Plan (Balanced - most popular)
- Silver Plan (High deductible, low premium, HSA-compatible)

**Eligibility:** Full-time employees (30+ hours/week) from day one

**Cost Examples (Gold Plan, US):**
- Employee Only: $45/month (you pay), $405/month (company pays)
- Family: $280/month (you pay), $840/month (company pays)

## Retirement & Financial

### 401(k) Plan (US) / Pension (EU/AU)

**Provider:** Fidelity (US), equivalent local providers

**Company Match:**
- 100% match up to 4% of salary
- 50% match on next 2% of salary
- **Total potential match: 5% if you contribute 6%**

**Vesting:** Immediate (100% vested from day one)

**Eligibility:** Full-time and part-time employees after 3 months

**Example:**
- Salary: $60,000
- You contribute: 6% ($3,600)
- Company contributes: 5% ($3,000)
- Total annual contribution: $6,600

---

### Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP)

**Eligible:** After 6 months of employment

**Benefit:** Buy SoleStride stock at 15% discount

**How It Works:**
- Contribute 1-15% of salary per paycheck
- Every 6 months, accumulated funds used to buy stock at 15% off
- No lock-up period (can sell immediately)

**Example:**
- Stock price: $20/share
- Your price: $17/share (15% discount)
- Instant 17.6% gain if you sell immediately

---

### Life Insurance

**Company-Provided:**
- Coverage: 2x annual salary (up to $500k max)
- Cost: $0 (fully covered)
- Beneficiary: You designate

**Voluntary Additional:**
- Purchase up to 5x salary
- Discounted group rates
- Covers spouse and children too

---

### Disability Insurance

**Short-Term Disability:**
- Coverage: 60% of salary for up to 6 months
- Waiting period: 7 days
- Cost: Fully covered by company

**Long-Term Disability:**
- Coverage: 60% of salary until age 65
- Waiting period: 6 months
- Cost: Fully covered by company
