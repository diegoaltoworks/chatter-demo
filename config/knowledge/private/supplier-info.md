## Supplier Payment Terms

### Standard Terms:
- Net 60 for established suppliers (6+ months relationship)
- Net 30 for new suppliers
- 50/50 split (deposit/delivery) for high-value orders ($100k+)

### Early Payment Discounts:
- 2% discount for Net 10
- 1% discount for Net 30
- We take advantage of these when cash flow permits
