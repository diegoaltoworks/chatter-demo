## Returns & Damaged Inventory

### Returns Processing

**Avg Monthly Returns:** 2,800 pairs (3.2% of sales - industry avg is 8-10%)

**Return Disposition:**
1. **Like New (35%)** - Restock and sell as new
2. **Gently Used (40%)** - Clean and sell through ReStride ReNew
3. **Well Worn (20%)** - Recycle through ReStride program
4. **Damaged/Defective (5%)** - Send to quality team, then recycle

**Processing Location:** All returns route to Portland first, then distributed

**Returns Backlog:** Currently 4 days (target: 3 days)

### Damaged Inventory

**Warehouse Damage Rate:** 0.4% (very low)
**In-Transit Damage Rate:** 0.8%

**Damage Types:**
- Crushed boxes (cosmetic) - discount 15%, sell as-is
- Minor scuffs - clean and sell through ReStride
- Sole separation or major defects - recycle
- Water damage - always recycle (quality concern)

**Damaged Inventory Write-Offs (Monthly):** ~$8,500
