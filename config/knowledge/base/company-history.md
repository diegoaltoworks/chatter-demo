# SoleStride Company History

## The Beginning (2018)

SoleStride was founded in 2018 by Maya Chen and David Rodriguez, two former Nike engineers who became frustrated with the footwear industry's environmental impact and wasteful practices.

### The Garage Days

Maya and David started in Maya's garage in Portland, Oregon, with a simple goal: create a running shoe using 100% sustainable materials without compromising performance. After 18 months and 47 prototypes, they launched their first product - the CloudRunner Zero.

## Key Milestones

### 2018 - Launch
- October: CloudRunner Zero launches via Kickstarter
- Raised $450,000 from 3,200 backers
- First 5,000 pairs ship in December

### 2019 - Growth
- January: Opens first retail store in Portland
- June: Expands product line with the UrbanStride casual sneaker
- September: Raises $5M Series A from Sequoia Capital
- December: Reaches 50,000 pairs sold

### 2020 - Scaling Up
- March: Pivots to direct-to-consumer during pandemic
- June: Launches women's-specific collection
- August: Opens manufacturing facility in Portugal
- November: Achieves B Corp certification
- Revenue: $12M

### 2021 - Expansion
- February: Launches hiking boot line (TrailBlazer series)
- May: Opens flagship store in New York City
- July: Introduces children's footwear
- October: Expands to European markets
- Revenue: $35M

### 2022 - Innovation
- January: Launches industry-first carbon-negative shoe (EcoStep)
- April: Partnership with Patagonia for collaborative collection
- June: Opens US manufacturing facility in Maine
- August: Achieves Climate Neutral certification
- November: Launches subscription service "SoleSubscribe"
- Revenue: $78M

### 2023 - Going Global
- March: Expands to Japan and Australia
- May: Introduces performance running line for athletes
- July: Opens 15 new retail locations
- October: Raises $25M Series B from Index Ventures
- Revenue: $142M

### 2024 - Present
- January: Launches repair and resale program
- April: Introduces vegan leather dress shoe line
- June: Partners with carbon removal platform Climeworks
- Current: On track for $200M+ annual revenue

## Innovation Timeline

Throughout our history, we've pioneered several industry firsts:

- **2018**: First running shoe made from 95% recycled ocean plastic
- **2020**: Developed proprietary algae-based foam cushioning
- **2021**: First fully biodegradable sneaker (decomposes in 90 days)
- **2022**: Carbon-negative manufacturing process
- **2023**: Waterless dyeing technology
- **2024**: Shoe-as-a-service subscription model

## Looking Forward

We're committed to achieving:
- 100% renewable energy in all facilities by 2025
- Zero waste to landfill by 2026
- Carbon negative across entire supply chain by 2027
- 50% recycled content in all products by 2025
