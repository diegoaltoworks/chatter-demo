# About SoleStride

SoleStride is a sustainable footwear company founded in 2018, dedicated to creating comfortable, stylish shoes that don't cost the earth.

## Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves shoes that feel good and do good. Our mission is to craft high-quality, sustainable footwear while maintaining ethical manufacturing practices and reducing our environmental impact.

## What We Do

SoleStride designs and manufactures premium footwear with a focus on:

- **Sustainability** - Using recycled and renewable materials
- **Comfort** - Biomechanically designed for all-day wear
- **Style** - Modern designs that don't sacrifice aesthetics
- **Ethics** - Fair wages and safe working conditions throughout our supply chain

## Our Values

1. **Environmental Stewardship** - We're committed to carbon neutrality by 2025
2. **Transparency** - We publish detailed information about our manufacturing and materials
3. **Quality Over Quantity** - We make shoes that last, not fast fashion
4. **Community** - We give back 1% of revenue to environmental causes
5. **Innovation** - Constantly researching new sustainable materials and processes

## Company Size

- Headquarters: Portland, Oregon
- Employees: 450+ globally
- Manufacturing facilities: 3 (Vietnam, Portugal, USA)
- Retail locations: 25 stores across North America and Europe
- Annual production: ~2 million pairs

## Recognition

- B Corp Certified since 2020
- Winner of Sustainable Fashion Award 2022
- Featured in Forbes "Most Innovative Sustainable Brands"
