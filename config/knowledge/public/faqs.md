No! We provide a prepaid return label for all US returns. First international return is also free.

### Can I exchange for a different size?

Yes! Size exchanges are free and fast. We ship the new size immediately, and you return the original pair using our prepaid label.

### What if I wore the shoes and they don't fit?

That's fine! Our 60-day guarantee allows you to wear the shoes. As long as they're not excessively worn or damaged, you can return them.

## Product Questions

### Are SoleStride shoes true to size?

Most styles run true to size, but it varies:
- **Running shoes (CloudRunner):** True to size
- **Casual shoes (UrbanStride):** Slightly large - many people size down
- **Hiking boots (TrailBlazer):** Size up half a size for thick socks

Check our detailed sizing guide for each product.

### Do you offer wide width shoes?

Our standard width accommodates most feet. CloudRunner and UrbanStride work well for wider feet. For custom widths, contact us for bulk/corporate orders (50+ pairs).

### Are your shoes vegan?

Most SoleStride shoes are vegan! Products to avoid if you're vegan:
- Select styles use merino wool (CloudRunner, some UrbanStride colors)
- Some insoles use cork

Check product descriptions - we clearly mark materials. Our entire Dress Professional line uses apple leather (vegan).

### How do I clean my SoleStride shoes?

**UrbanStride:** Machine washable! Use cold water, gentle cycle, air dry.

**CloudRunner & other shoes:**
1. Remove insoles and laces
2. Hand wash with mild soap and water
3. Use soft brush for stubborn dirt
4. Air dry (never put in dryer or direct sunlight)
5. Stuff with newspaper to maintain shape

Never use bleach or harsh chemicals.

### How long do SoleStride shoes last?

With proper care:
- **Running shoes:** 400-500 miles (typical for any running shoe)
- **Casual shoes:** 2-3 years of regular wear
- **Hiking boots:** 500-800 miles on trail

We offer a 2-year warranty on manufacturing defects. Many customers report their shoes lasting much longer!

### Can shoes be repaired?

Yes! Our dress shoes and hiking boots are designed to be resoled. Contact us for repair services, or take them to a local cobbler. We also have a repair program through our retail stores.

## Sustainability

### Are SoleStride shoes really sustainable?

Yes! Every pair is made with a minimum of 75% recycled or renewable materials. We're carbon neutral (certified), and our EcoStep model is actually carbon negative.

Full transparency: We publish detailed sustainability reports annually. Check solestride.com/impact.

### What happens to my old shoes?

Through our ReStride program:
- We clean and repair gently-used shoes for resale
- We recycle worn-out shoes into new materials
- Nothing goes to landfill - 100% recycling rate
- You get $20 credit for each pair returned

### What does "carbon neutral" mean?

We measure all carbon emissions from materials, manufacturing, shipping, and operations. We reduce emissions where possible, then offset the rest through verified carbon removal projects (tree planting, direct air capture, renewable energy).

Net result: Zero carbon footprint.

### What does "carbon negative" mean (EcoStep)?

Our EcoStep shoes remove more carbon from the atmosphere than they create. Each pair removes 5kg of CO2 (costs 2kg to make = -3kg net). We partner with Climeworks for direct air capture.
