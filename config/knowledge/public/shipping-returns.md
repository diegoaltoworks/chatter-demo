# Shipping & Returns

## Shipping Information

### Domestic Shipping (United States)

**Standard Shipping - FREE on orders over $100**
- Cost: $8 (orders under $100)
- Delivery: 5-7 business days
- Carrier: USPS or UPS Ground

**Express Shipping**
- Cost: $18
- Delivery: 2-3 business days
- Carrier: UPS 2nd Day Air

**Overnight Shipping**
- Cost: $35
- Delivery: 1 business day
- Carrier: UPS Next Day Air
- Order by 2 PM PT for same-day processing

### International Shipping

We ship to over 45 countries worldwide.

**Canada**
- Standard: $15 (7-10 business days)
- Express: $35 (3-5 business days)
- Duties & taxes calculated at checkout

**Europe (UK, EU, Switzerland)**
- Standard: $20 (10-14 business days)
- Express: $45 (5-7 business days)
- DDP (duties paid) - no surprise fees

**Australia & New Zealand**
- Standard: $25 (12-16 business days)
- Express: $50 (6-8 business days)

**Asia (Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong)**
- Standard: $22 (10-15 business days)
- Express: $48 (5-7 business days)

**Note:** All international orders ship via DHL or FedEx

### Order Processing

- **Processing time:** 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays)
- **Order cutoff:** 2 PM Pacific Time for same-day processing
- **Weekends:** Orders placed Friday after 2 PM through Sunday process on Monday
- **Tracking:** Automatically sent via email when your order ships
- **Packaging:** 100% recycled and recyclable materials

### Shipping Restrictions

We cannot ship to:
- PO Boxes for express/overnight orders (standard shipping OK)
- APO/FPO addresses (contact customer service for alternatives)
- Freight forwarding addresses

## Returns & Exchanges

### Our 60-Day Guarantee

Try your SoleStride shoes risk-free for 60 days. If you're not completely satisfied, return or exchange them - we cover shipping both ways.

### Return Eligibility

**What's Returnable:**
- Unworn shoes in original packaging with tags attached
- Gently worn shoes within 60 days (Yes, you can wear them!)
- Defective or damaged items (any time within 2 years - see warranty)

**What's NOT Returnable:**
- Shoes worn beyond normal "try-on" wear after 60 days
- Customized or personalized items
- Items marked as final sale (rare, clearly noted)
- Shoes damaged through misuse or neglect

### How to Return

**Method 1: Online Return Portal (Easiest)**
1. Visit solestride.com/returns
2. Enter order number and email
3. Select items to return
4. Print prepaid return label
5. Drop off at any UPS location or USPS box

**Method 2: In-Store Return**
- Visit any SoleStride retail location
- Bring your shoes and order confirmation
- Get immediate refund or exchange

**Method 3: Contact Customer Service**
- Email: returns@solestride.com
- Phone: 1-800-SOLE-123
- We'll guide you through the process

### Return Shipping

**Domestic (US):**
- **Free return shipping** - we provide a prepaid label
- No deductions from your refund

**International:**
- **First return free** - we provide a prepaid label
- Additional returns: $15 deducted from refund
- Or use your own carrier and email us tracking

### Exchanges

**Free Size/Color Exchanges:**
- Same product, different size or color = FREE
- We ship the new pair immediately
- Return the original pair using provided label
- No charge to your card

**Different Product:**
- Treated as return + new order
- Return original for refund
- Place new order online

**Exchange Process:**
1. Start exchange at solestride.com/exchange
2. Select new size/color
3. New pair ships within 24 hours
4. Return original within 30 days

### Refunds

**Refund Timeline:**
- Refund processed within 3 business days of receiving return
- Credit card refunds: 5-7 business days to appear
- Original payment method only
- Shipping fees refunded if item is defective

**Partial Refunds:**
If shoes show excessive wear beyond try-on:
- We may issue partial refund (rare, case-by-case)
- You'll be notified before refund is processed
- You can request shoes be returned to you instead

## Warranty

### 2-Year Quality Guarantee

We stand behind our products. If your SoleStride shoes develop defects in materials or workmanship within 2 years, we'll replace them free of charge.

**Covered:**
- Sole separation
- Upper material tears or defects
- Seam failures
- Hardware breakage (eyelets, laces, closures)

**Not Covered:**
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from misuse or accidents
- Improper care or cleaning
- Unauthorized modifications

**How to File a Warranty Claim:**
1. Email photos to warranty@solestride.com
2. Include order number and description
3. Our team reviews within 2 business days
4. Approved claims receive free replacement
