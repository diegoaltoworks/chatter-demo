# Sustainability at SoleStride

## Our Commitment

At SoleStride, we believe the footwear industry needs to change. We're committed to proving that you don't have to sacrifice quality, comfort, or style to make environmentally responsible choices.

## Environmental Goals

### 2025 Targets
- ✅ **Carbon Neutral** - Achieved in 2022 (3 years early!)
- 🎯 **50% Recycled Materials** in all products
- 🎯 **100% Renewable Energy** in all facilities
- 🎯 **Zero Waste to Landfill** in manufacturing

### 2027 Targets
- 🎯 **Carbon Negative** across entire supply chain
- 🎯 **Circular Economy** model (take back and recycle all shoes)
- 🎯 **Water Neutral** operations
- 🎯 **Living Wage** for all workers globally

## Materials Innovation

### What We Use

**Recycled Materials:**
- **Ocean Plastic** - Each CloudRunner uses 12 recycled bottles
- **Recycled Rubber** - Our outsoles contain 30-40% recycled content
- **Recycled Cotton** - From textile waste, not virgin crops
- **Recycled Polyester** - 86% less carbon than virgin polyester

**Renewable Materials:**
- **Merino Wool** - From ethically-raised sheep in New Zealand
- **AlgaeFoam™** - Cushioning made from harvested algae blooms
- **SugarFoam™** - Midsoles from sugarcane (carbon-negative crop)
- **Cork** - Renewable, biodegradable insole material
- **Hemp** - Fast-growing, low-water alternative to cotton
- **Eucalyptus Fiber** - Leather alternative for hiking boots
- **Apple Leather** - Made from apple pomace waste

**Bio-Based Materials:**
- **Natural Rubber** - Sustainably tapped from rubber trees
- **Castor Bean Oil** - Bio-based coating alternative

### What We Don't Use

- ❌ Virgin petroleum-based plastics
- ❌ PVC or PU foam
- ❌ Chrome-tanned leather (uses toxic chemicals)
- ❌ Conventional cotton (high water and pesticide use)
- ❌ Synthetic down
- ❌ PFAS "forever chemicals"

## Manufacturing Process

### Carbon Footprint

**Average Industry Shoe:** 13.6 kg CO2e per pair
**SoleStride Average:** 5.2 kg CO2e per pair (62% reduction)
**EcoStep Model:** -3 kg CO2e per pair (carbon negative!)

### How We Achieve This:

**Renewable Energy:**
- Portugal facility: 100% solar powered
- Vietnam facility: 85% renewable (goal: 100% by 2025)
- Maine facility: 100% wind powered

**Efficient Production:**
- Waterless dyeing technology (saves 20L water per pair)
- Single-layer construction reduces waste
- 3D knitting minimizes fabric waste (95% efficiency vs. 60% industry standard)
- Algorithmic cutting reduces material waste by 15%

**Local Manufacturing:**
- 30% of shoes made in USA (Maine facility)
- 40% made in Portugal (closer to European customers)
- 30% made in Vietnam (solar-powered facility)
- Reduces shipping distances by average 4,000 miles

### Worker Welfare

**Fair Labor:**
- Living wages (150% of minimum wage minimum)
- Safe working conditions (regular third-party audits)
- Maximum 48-hour work weeks
- No child labor, forced labor, or discrimination
- Workers can unionize

**Benefits:**
- Health insurance for all workers
- Paid maternity/paternity leave
- On-site childcare (Vietnam and Portugal facilities)
- Skills training and education programs

### Transparency

- Full supplier list published on our website
- Annual sustainability report (third-party verified)
- Factory tours available by appointment
- Material sourcing details on every product page

## End of Life

### ReStride Take-Back Program

**How It Works:**
1. Return any SoleStride shoes (any condition)
2. We clean, repair, and resell (SoleStride ReNew)
3. Unrepairable shoes are recycled into new products
4. Get $20 credit toward your next purchase

**Impact:**
- Launched in 2024
- Goal: Take back 50,000 pairs in first year
- Current: 8,300 pairs returned (as of June 2024)
- Recycling rate: 100% (nothing goes to landfill)

### What Happens to Returned Shoes:

**Gently Used (40%):**
- Cleaned and sanitized
- Sold through SoleStride ReNew at 40-60% off
- 1-year warranty included

**Well-Worn but Repairable (30%):**
- Professionally repaired
- Outsoles replaced if needed
- Sold through SoleStride ReNew at 50-70% off

**End of Life (30%):**
- Deconstructed into component materials
- Rubber recycled into playground surfaces and track materials
- Foam recycled into carpet padding
- Textiles recycled into new fiber or insulation
- Metal components melted down for reuse

### Biodegradable Options

**UrbanStride Classic:**
- 90-day industrial composting
- Shoes break down into non-toxic biomass
- Available at participating composting facilities

**EcoStep:**
- Fully biodegradable in 180 days
- Can be home composted (requires shredding)
- Becomes nutrient-rich soil
