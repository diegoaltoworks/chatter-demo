# SoleStride Sizing Guide

## How to Measure Your Feet

For the most accurate fit, measure your feet at the end of the day when they're slightly swollen.

### You'll Need:
- A piece of paper larger than your foot
- A pen or pencil
- A ruler or measuring tape
- A wall

### Steps:

1. **Prepare:** Wear the socks you'll typically wear with your shoes
2. **Position:** Place the paper on a hard floor against a wall
3. **Stand:** Place your heel against the wall on the paper
4. **Mark:** Have someone mark where your longest toe ends
5. **Measure:** Measure from the wall to the mark in inches or centimeters
6. **Repeat:** Measure both feet (they're often slightly different!)
7. **Use the larger measurement** for sizing

## Fit Guide by Product

### CloudRunner Zero & Running Shoes
**Recommended Fit:** Snug but not tight. You should have about a thumb's width between your longest toe and the end of the shoe. These run true to size.

- **Narrow feet:** Consider going down half a size or using our included heel pads
- **Wide feet:** Order your normal size - our running shoes have a roomy toe box
- **High arches:** True to size works well with our supportive insoles

### UrbanStride Classic & Casual Shoes
**Recommended Fit:** These run slightly large. If you're between sizes, size down. The merino wool will conform to your foot over time.

- **First-time buyers:** 85% order their normal size and report excellent fit
- **Break-in period:** Minimal - comfortable from day one
- **Wide feet:** Normal size provides plenty of room

### TrailBlazer Hiking Boots
**Recommended Fit:** Size up half a size from your normal shoe. You need extra room for thicker socks and foot swelling during long hikes.

- **Thick hiking socks:** Definitely size up half a size
- **Downhill hiking:** Extra toe room prevents black toenails
- **Recommendation:** 90% of customers size up half a size

### Dress Professional
**Recommended Fit:** True to size. These fit similarly to traditional leather dress shoes.

## Width Options

Most SoleStride shoes come in standard (D for men, B for women) width. For particularly narrow or wide feet:

- **Narrow feet (A/B width):** CloudRunner Zero and UrbanStride work well
- **Wide feet (E/2E width):** Contact our customer service for recommendations
- **Custom widths:** Available for bulk/corporate orders (50+ pairs)

## Pro Tips

1. **Measure annually:** Feet can change size over time
2. **Time of day matters:** Feet swell throughout the day
3. **Try before you buy:** Walk around your house for 15 minutes
4. **Sock thickness:** Affects fit significantly
5. **Between sizes?** Generally size up for athletic shoes, down for casual
